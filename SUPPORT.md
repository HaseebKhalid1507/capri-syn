# Getting help

- **Installing or running her:** start with [docs/INSTALL.md](docs/INSTALL.md) and its Known issues.
  Windows specifics are in [docs/WINDOWS.md](docs/WINDOWS.md), voice in [docs/VOICE.md](docs/VOICE.md).
- **Questions and ideas:** [Discussions](https://github.com/HaseebKhalid1507/capri-syn/discussions).
- **Something's broken:** [open a bug report](https://github.com/HaseebKhalid1507/capri-syn/issues/new/choose)
  and attach her log:
  - Linux: `~/.local/state/synaps-avatar/launch.log`
  - Windows: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\synaps-avatar\synaps-avatar.log`
- **Security issues:** privately, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

capri-syn is a fan project maintained in spare time. Replies may take a few days.
