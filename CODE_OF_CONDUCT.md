# Code of Conduct

capri-syn is a small fan project. We want everyone who turns up here, to report a bug, ask a
question or send a patch, to have a good time doing it.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Assume good faith.
- Give credit where it's due: to contributors, and to the artists whose work makes Capri possible.
- Accept feedback on your work gracefully, and give it the same way.

## What isn't OK

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualised content or unwelcome attention.
- Discrimination of any kind: on age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression,
  experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions, or anything else that would make a reasonable person feel unwelcome.

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere someone represents
the project.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainer, [@HaseebKhalid1507](https://github.com/HaseebKhalid1507). To report privately,
use the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form and say it's a conduct report; only
the maintainer sees it. GitHub's own *Report content* also reaches GitHub staff. Reports are kept
confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and
may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who does, depending on what happened.
